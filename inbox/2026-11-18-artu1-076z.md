Counts and method only. Ruling by Sunny on 18 Nov 2026; each line gives the reason.

- **Asked to finish within one to two days; the registered window stays as it is.** The window's length and the
  spacing between pull requests were fixed before any result. Shortening them would cut the re-triggers for the later
  cases (lowering the review tools' completion, so measuring something else), risk the hourly rate limit a rehearsal
  measured, and need a new freeze with every check repeated, which would itself cost about a day. Reason: a faster
  answer to a different question is not worth a disputable result.
- **What was sped up instead:** the blind hand-check starts right after the window closes, the snapshot is hashed and
  the judges have run (about 90 minutes, an estimate), and continues the same day, with a second day only if needed;
  nothing is shown to the hand-checker until the last item. The write-up is drafted during the window as a skeleton
  where every number and every outcome-dependent sentence is a placeholder, from the registered templates only. Reason:
  both save about a day and change no registered rule.
